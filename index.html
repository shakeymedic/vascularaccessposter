Vascular Access in the Resus Room

Summary of the German S1 Guideline (2025) for Adult Emergency Patients

Struck et al. Int J Emerg Med (2025) 18:187

A. General Standards of Care

Peripheral & IO Access

  • ✓Secure 2x large-bore cannulas (≥18G, >100ml/min).
  • ✓Functional pre-hospital lines are valid for use.
  • !Cardiac Arrest: Immediate IO if IV fails rapidly.
  • •Replace non-aseptic emergency lines within 24 hours.

Vasopressor Safety

  • ✓Peripheral acceptable for initial stabilisation.
  • •Conditions: Confirmed intravascular, low concentration, calm limb.
  • →Switch to CVC if high dosage needed or instability persists post-CT.

CVC & Arterial Lines

  • !Do not delay initial diagnostics (CT) for lines in stable patients.
  • ✓Mandate Ultrasound guidance (CVC & Art).
  • •Verify tip: Atrial swirl sign (Echo), Endovascular ECG, or CT.
  • •Suture all CVCs and Femoral Art lines.

Hygiene & Checks

  • ✕No prophylactic antibiotics for insertion.
  • •Document insertion depth clearly.
  • ✓Aspirate and flush all lumens with sterile 0.9% NaCl.

B. Clinical Scenario Matrix

Polytrauma

C-Spine Collar:
Avoid Internal Jugular (IJ). Impractical.

Chest Trauma/Tube:
Place Subclavian CVC on the injured side.

Pelvic Binder:
Ipsilateral femoral double puncture (Art + Vein) is viable.

Neuro / ICP

Positioning:
Maintain 15-30° head-up tilt, even during CVC placement.

Vessel Choice:
Internal Jugular is SAFE. No clear evidence it impairs venous outflow.

Lysis / Anticoag

Warning:
Avoid Subclavian (compressible site required).

Recommendation:
Use Left Femoral vein.

Reserve Right Femoral for Angio.

Respiratory Failure

Avoid Subclavian. High risk of iatrogenic pneumothorax. Prefer IJ or Femoral. If SCV unavoidable, use US (axillary approach).

Sepsis

Guide fluid therapy via Art/CVC monitoring. Insert dialysis catheter in Resus if hyperkalaemia or acute renal failure present.

Cardiac Arrest

Prioritise Art line for monitoring (do not delay CPR). Patients with IO only must receive peripheral IV eventually.
